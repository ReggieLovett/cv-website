# Companion move and appearance references

The companion has **13 selectable forms**, each with **ten battle techniques**. There are **129 distinct techniques across 130 form assignments** because Sage Mode and Six Paths KCM share Rasenshuriken. Each performance still chooses one to three moves. All powers keep the active persona’s original head, body, proportions, and native animation frames. Root travel is horizontal along the bottom; seven forms add a low local hover. Yuno is one combined Spirit Dive + Star Magic choice. Shadow Clone is no longer a standalone form, and Noelle’s Leviathan Dragon Armor replaces Frost Armor.

## Additional OFA and knight techniques

**Delaware Smash: Air Force** projects compressed air from Deku’s fingers: the [official episode 85 synopsis](https://heroaca.com/episodes/4th/ep85/) identifies Air Force in the Gentle fight. Its visual is a short series of pale pressure rings, rather than a fireball or Rasengan. **Blackchain** combines Blackwhip and Fa Jin for a reinforced binding and throw; the [chapter-cited technique reference](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Blackchain) identifies chapter 368 / episode 151. The [licensed broadcaster’s episode 165 account](https://times.abema.tv/en/articles/-/10208331?page=1) also describes the combination in the later battle. The sprite shows dark links with green edges and a red kinetic accent closing into a pull. The ten-move repertoire retains the five active inherited-quirk techniques, static OFA energy and Float.

The three latest OFA moves have distinct physical cues: **Detroit Smash: Quintuple** stages five accelerated fist impacts; **Faux 100%: Manchester Smash** releases a Blackwhip tether into a descending armored axe kick; **St. Louis Smash: Air Force** sweeps a boot through a broad travelling pressure wave. The [licensed Crunchyroll episode 151 clip](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VXScmLyECUM) and [official episode 151](https://www.heroaca.com/episodes/7th/ep151/) anchor the Final War sequence. [Bandai’s OFA developer notes](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/my-hero-academia/news/my-hero-ultra-rumble-developers-blog-vol-22) confirm its ranged, high-speed, Gearshift, Smokescreen and Fa Jin mechanics. The [official St. Louis/Air Force gameplay notes](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/my-hero-academia/news/my-hero-ultra-rumble-developers-blog-vol-7) verify those named attacks; the episode 134 Manchester timing and the exact named combinations are cross-checked against chapter-cited [Manchester](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Faux_100_Percent%3A_Manchester_Smash), [Quintuple](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Detroit_Smash%3A_Quintuple), and [St. Louis Air Force](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/St._Louis_Smash_Air_Force) secondary references. Their compact pixel effects adapt the described shapes; the knight does not copy Deku’s body.

Draw Sword has ten original knight techniques. Whirling Blade, Shield Riposte, Piercing Lunge, Rising Cut, Pommel Strike, Blade Beam and Deflect & Counter extend the quickdraw, cross cut and guard break. The motions distinguish circular cuts, a shield catch, a narrow thrust, an upward sword swing, a close pommel jab, a released blade crescent and a parry followed by a return cut. They carry no invented anime-spell attribution.

## Final War Deku

The user-selected appearance is Final War / One For All Full Overdrive Deku. The [official episode 151 synopsis](https://heroaca.com/episodes/7th/ep151/) places the second inheritor’s ability in Deku’s confrontation with Shigaraki. Its [official promotional still](https://www.heroaca.com/wp/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/movie-1.jpg) supplies the ragged yellow scarf, dark neck guard, and mint-colored energy spikes. Costume layers retain the original sprite body, with green One For All energy, Blackwhip accents, and a 2.8-second charge/release. A small body hover evokes Float while the actor remains in the bottom lane. The attack labels describe small sprite performances of the referenced abilities; the full repertoire also represents Danger Sense and Smokescreen as support techniques.

### Luck: True Lightning, with Ultimate Magic kept separate

The chapter-cited [Ceranos reference](https://blackclover.fandom.com/wiki/Keraunos) gives the VIZ/Funimation English name and places the technique in chapter 248 and **episode 163**. Ceranos draws natural mana through five arrays beneath equipped feet to propel Luck as lightning. Its color and pixel simplification are costume/effect adaptations. Luck stays grounded between bursts; short aerial propulsion is not presented as a sustained floating idle.

### Yuno: complete Spirit Dive and Saint Stage

Complete Spirit Dive and Saint Stage are related milestones. The chooser is **Spirit Dive + Star Magic**, with internal ID `spirit-dive`. It combines Yuno’s complete Spirit Dive/Saint cues with his four star anchors and two Clover/Spade grimoires; this does not assert a separately named canonical costume. Five spirit/wind techniques and five star techniques share this one form. Conjunction’s counter also incorporates the evasive wind choreography of Spirit’s Hushed Dance, rather than occupying two additional move slots. The chapter-cited [secondary Spirit Dive description](https://blackclover.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_Dive) places the complete form in chapter 276: spirit mana covers both sides, the half-crown becomes a full crown of connected crosses, a pair of wings replaces the single wing, and a circle of connected crosses appears behind the back. The [official anime teaser announcement](https://bclover.jp/news/detail.php?id=1130200) also identifies its pictured Yuno as Spirit Dive.

Yuno reaches Saint Stage in chapter 310 and channels it into Saint Spirit of Zephyr; it is not exclusive to Noelle. The [licensed VIZ chapter](https://www.viz.com/shonenjump/black-clover-chapter-310/chapter/23457) requires a subscription; the accessible account is the chapter-cited [secondary chapter synopsis](https://blackclover.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_310). Noelle’s Undine/Valkyrie crown, halo, armor, and lance should not be copied onto Yuno. For this companion, the complete cross-crown, two wind wings, cross-circle, green/white energy, and gold costume accents sit over the original sprite. The cloak and simplified palette are costume adaptations, not a claim that an all-white fan illustration is an official separate “Saint Yuno” design.

Saint Stage adds a combat property to that completed spirit partnership. The chapter 310 synopsis describes Yuno approaching full resonance with Sylph, forming Saint Spirit of Zephyr, and cutting through Zenon’s body and devil heart. Its meaningful visual distinction is a focused, brilliant wind-sword edge and a clean impact bloom, while complete Spirit Dive already supplies the crown, paired wings, and mana covering. A compact white/emerald blade core, brighter edge, and brief dissipating impact sparks communicate the Saint strike without replacing the knight, repeating the transformation, or borrowing a fan-made angel costume. These small effect shapes are an adaptation of the described technique, not an independently verified new official costume.

## Motion and lifecycle

The standalone Shadow Clone form and its generic attack pool are removed. Naruto’s sourced clone-based combat techniques remain within Naruto’s own forms. Noelle’s water form uses dark dragon-horn/fin ornaments, scale details, layered silver/blue water armor, back plumes, a spiked water-tail accent and a spiraling lance on the same knight; it does not switch to a Noelle body. The [licensed Tong Li publisher panel](https://ebook.tongli.com.tw/news_content?id=427ea8a8-9753-45b8-310d-08dbfaea83ae) shows the Dragon Form’s darker pointed head pieces, spiked limbs and plume-like armor; the blue/silver palette and compact tail are creative knight adaptations. See [the publisher and chapter references](companion-clover-references.md).

All combinations contain one to three moves. New effect modules preserve the existing bottom clip, facing, pause/hidden/control holds, chat/reset/resize cancellation and reduced-motion behavior. Seven choices use a low hover: Six Paths KCM, OFA Full Overdrive, Hyper Sonic, Devil Union, Spirit Dive + Star Magic, Hellfire Incarnate and Leviathan Dragon Armor. Projectiles are bounded to the existing mobile battle reserve; root Y remains zero. Reduced motion applies costume layers without hover or combat motion.

## Ranged attacks, Mana Zone and equipment

Ranged attacks separate from the caster: Rasenshurikens spin and travel, Tailed Beast Bombs charge then release, flame/lightning orbs cross the lane, Air Force expands outward, Euros draws a bow and releases an arrow, and Quartile Hasta projects a four-star beam. Hyper’s Boom, Cross Slash and Homing Shot preserve their different wave, crossed-wave and curved-orb paths. Support moves retain their mechanic: Amaterasu ignites at the target rather than becoming a projectile; barriers surround/protect; Danger Sense warns and evades.

Mereoleona’s Mana Zone barrage, Purgatory: Abyss, Crossfire application and Zone Press application show an enclosing dome, elliptical ground boundary and six surrounding mana marks. Yuno’s Never-Never Land uses that boundary in emerald alongside its star field. These are compact illustrations of area control, fitting the existing bottom combat lane. Their boundaries appear before the attack and fade with it. Sources and the distinction between canon spells and creative applications are in the [Clover notes](companion-clover-references.md).

Asta equips the sword named by the technique: Demon-Slayer is a broad, blunt slab with a dark edge; Demon-Dweller is slimmer with an inset pattern; Demon-Destroyer has a distinctive ornate guard; Demon-Slasher is a narrow katana. Bull Thrust brings both Slayer and Dweller. Black Divider extends the Slayer silhouette. Yuno’s Zephyr is a luminous wind sword, Boreas is a halberd and Euros is a bow; no idle wind sword remains visible during unrelated star casts. Sasuke’s idle weapon is a straight Kusanagi-style blade, while Susano’o Sword Slash uses its larger purple spectral blade. Noelle uses water armor’s spiraling lance for her lance attacks. The original Draw Sword kit retains its adventurer blade. Equipment is drawn as small code-native layers and never changes either persona’s source sprite.

## Susano’o and Kurama attacks

The Sasuke form summons a purple armored Susano’o for Sword Slash, Flame Control and Indra’s Arrow. Six Paths KCM summons a gold, nine-tailed Kurama avatar for Chakra Arm Barrage, Tailed Beast Bomb, Super Tailed Beast Rasen Shuriken and Tailed Beast Barrage. Its bomb spawns at the fox’s muzzle before releasing. These are transient constructs around the same original knight/elf, not full replacements or new selectable forms. All thirteen forms still have ten techniques each. See [the primary sources, game/anime distinctions and avatar mechanics](companion-naruto-references.md).
